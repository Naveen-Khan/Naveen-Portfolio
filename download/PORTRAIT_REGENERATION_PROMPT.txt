============================================================================
PROMPT (copy everything below the line)
============================================================================

Editorial fashion portrait photograph of a young South Asian woman
wearing a soft black niqab and matching abaya with subtle tone-on-tone
embroidery, only her calm, intelligent eyes visible, looking directly
at the camera with a composed, confident, professional expression.

She is photographed against a seamless warm ivory backdrop (color
#F5F1E8, slightly cream, not pure white) with soft natural studio
lighting from the upper-left, gentle gradient shadow falling to the
lower-right of her frame, no harsh highlights, no greenish tint from
any outdoor environment.

Wardrobe: minimalist matte black abaya and matching niqab in a soft
matte fabric (not shiny), with restrained tone-on-tone embroidery
near the neckline, no ID badges, no logos, no jewelry visible, no
text on clothing.

Crop: framed from mid-torso upward, head and shoulders centered,
subject occupies about 70% of the frame height, generous negative
space above the head and to one side.

Mood: editorial, calm, intelligent, premium, like a high-end magazine
profile shot (think Vogue portrait editorial, Aperture magazine,
Time magazine person of the year portrait — composed, dignified, not
glamorous, not AI-looking, no futuristic elements, no glow, no neon,
no circuit-board patterns, no robots, no AI-themed iconography).

Style: shot on Hasselblad medium format, 85mm lens at f/2.8, natural
window light, subtle film grain, soft global contrast, slightly
desaturated warm color grade (cream + black + faint warm gold
highlights), no HDR, no plastic skin, no over-sharpening.

Negative prompt: outdoor background, trees, garden, sun, ID badge,
logo, jewelry, smiling teeth, futuristic, neon, cyberpunk, robot,
AI brain illustration, glowing circuit, particles, blurry background,
shallow depth of field bokeh, Instagram filter, AI plastic skin,
exaggerated features, multiple subjects.

Aspect ratio: 4:5 portrait orientation.

============================================================================
TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS
============================================================================

1. Generate 8-12 variations, pick the one where:
   - the eyes look directly at the camera
   - the abaya falls cleanly without wrinkles or stray fabric
   - the background is uniformly cream/ivory (no gradients of green)
   - the expression is calm and intelligent (not smiling, not sad)

2. If the niqab fabric looks too shiny, add to the negative prompt:
   "shiny fabric, satin, glossy reflection, plastic sheen"

3. If the background has any green tint (from outdoor samples the AI
   may have learned), add to the negative prompt:
   "green background, foliage, trees, outdoor, garden, sun"

4. For final use in the portfolio hero arch:
   - Crop to a vertical 4:5 ratio
   - Apply a slight warm color grade (5-10K cooler than neutral,
     +5 saturation, +3 contrast)
   - Resize to 720×900 px or 1080×1350 px for retina
   - Save as PNG with transparent or solid cream (#E6DECC) background
     matching the inner arch fill color

5. File path to use after generation:
   /home/z/my-project/public/portfolio/portrait.png
   (replace the existing file with your generated version)
